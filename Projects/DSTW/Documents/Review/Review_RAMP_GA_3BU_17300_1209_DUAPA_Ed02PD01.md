# Review – RAMP_GA_3BU_17300_1209_DUAPA_Ed02PD01

**Document under review:** [RAMP_GA_3BU_17300_1209_DUAPA_Ed02PD01.docx](./RAMP_GA_3BU_17300_1209_DUAPA_Ed02PD01.docx)  
**Scope of review:** RAM Plan for Generic Application DIL OBB 1.0.0 and Solution Element VIL OBB 1.0.0; focus on scope, system description, redundancy, capacity assumptions and Chapter 6 RAM deliverables.  
**Conclusion:** The plan covers DIL and VIL jointly. Chapter 6.2 is incomplete if intended to list all applicable accepted availability requirements: it omits DIL-CU requirement 00567 and VIL requirements 00573 and 00574 in the available later SSS_RAMS edition. The four automatic-correction requirements in the plan are not present in that later edition and are marked Draft in the older SSS DIL edition; two listed requirement titles also differ from the later edition. The system description should be clarified: it contains repeated and partly conflicting statements about redundancy, and the stated capacity differs from the available GA SEMP. The claim that three Computing Nodes (CNs) can fail without operational impact is not supported by the VIL SSS reviewed. In Chapter 6, the deliverable types and their recommended/required status are consistent with the preceding RAM activity descriptions; however, the heading “Periodical RAM activities report” does not match the text beneath it, which describes RAM Plan revisions rather than periodic reporting.

The proposed replacement text is written as document-ready prose. Section 3.2 text replaces the corresponding repeated descriptions; retain the figures and use the short figure-introduction wording below. Configuration-specific statements remain subject to confirmation against the approved baseline, as noted under Limitations.

## Limitations and open confirmations

1. The maximum number of VILs/interlockings conflicts between the RAM Plan (435) and the available SEMP_GA (287). Neither source was assumed to take precedence; the document/requirements baseline owner should confirm the applicable figure.
2. The proposed statements that the VIL supports up to two redundant nodes in the basic configuration and up to four nodes across two sites in the georedundant configuration are taken from VIL SSS requirements marked Draft. Confirm that these configurations are adopted in the approved baseline before inserting them as definitive system descriptions. The georedundant ECMUX configuration is not finally approved in the available VIL SSS and is therefore not specified in the proposed replacement text.
3. The VIL SSS marks some redundancy requirements as Draft or In negotiation. The 1oo4 configuration implies function can continue with one CN remaining, but the available requirement explicitly states robustness against failures affecting one or two CNs, not a verified three-CN failure case. Confirm the three-CN case, including site-failure combinations and surviving-node capacity, through the approved architecture and system-level verification before treating it as demonstrated behaviour. Check all requirement statuses against the current approved baseline before releasing the RAM Plan.
4. Chapter 6.2 omits accepted requirements 00567, 00573 and 00574 in the available later SSS_RAMS edition. Confirm their applicability to this plan and include them if they are within the GA DIL/VIL RAM analysis boundary. The automatic-correction requirement IDs 00409–00410 and 00571–00572 are present in the older SSS DIL edition as Draft but absent from the later SSS_RAMS edition; confirm their current status before retaining them. Section 6.2 is an availability list, not a complete RAM requirement register; confirm that reliability and maintainability requirements are captured in the controlled requirements traceability and allocated to the appropriate analyses/deliverables.
